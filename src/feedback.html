Common Inquiries

Before reaching out, you might find the answer to your question below.

Do you offer vegetarian options?

Yes, we have a dedicated section on our menu for vegetarian and vegan meals.

How do the Daily Combo Deals work?

Combo deals are pre-set discounted meals updated every morning.

Can I pre-order my food online?

Currently, orders must be placed physically at the counter, but we are working on an online portal!

Who do I contact for lost items?

Please visit the counter or email us immediately at support@campusbites.ucp.edu.pk.
