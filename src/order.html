HOW TO ORDER

How to Order at Campus Bites

Hungry? Ordering is easy. Follow four simple steps and enjoy your favourite meals between lectures.

View Menu Today's Deals

Place Your Order in 4 Easy Steps

STEP 1

Choose Your Items

Explore the menu and special deals, then decide what you want.

STEP 2

Pay at the Counter

Visit the cash counter, tell the staff your order, and pay by cash or card. Keep your receipt safe.

STEP 3

Collect Your Order Token

Receive your token number with your receipt. Wait for your number to be called on the display board.

STEP 4

Collect Your Food

Show your token at the pickup counter, collect your food, and check your order before leaving.

Good to Know

Peak Hours

Our busiest time is 12:30 PM - 2:00 PM. Orders may take an extra 10-15 minutes at peak time.

Pick-up Rules

Collect your order within 15 minutes of your number being called, otherwise it may be kept aside.

Takeaway

Inform the counter staff in advance if you need takeaway packing.

Student Discount

Bring a valid student card when paying for the Student Combo.

Food Allergies

Inform our staff about any food allergies before placing your order.

Keep Your Receipt

Keep your receipt and token until you have collected your complete order.

Need Help With Your Order?

Have a question or suggestion? We are happy to help.
