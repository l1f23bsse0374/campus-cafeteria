How to Order Your Favorite Food

Hungry? Ordering at Campus Bites is easy! Follow these four simple steps and enjoy your favorite meals.

Explore Our Menu

Place Your Order in 4 Easy Steps

Follow these simple steps for a smooth ordering experience.

  1. 🍕

    Choose Your Items

    Explore our delicious menu and special deals. Choose your favorite food and decide what you want.

  2. 💳

    Pay at the Counter

    Visit the cash counter, tell our staff your order, and pay by cash or card. Keep your receipt safe.

  3. 🎟️

    Collect Your Order Token

    Receive your token number with your receipt. Wait for your number to be called on the display board.

  4. 🍔

    Collect Your Food

    Show your token at the pickup counter, collect your food, and check your order before leaving.

Good to Know

Keep these helpful tips in mind before placing your order.

  • ⏰
    Peak Hours

    Our busiest time is 12:30 PM - 2:00 PM. Orders may take an extra 10-15 minutes.

  • 🎫
    Pick-up Rules

    Collect your order within 15 minutes of your number being called.

  • 🥡
    Takeaway

    Inform the counter staff in advance if you need takeaway packing.

  • 🎓
    Student Discount

    Bring a valid student card when paying for the Student Combo.

  • ⚠️
    Food Allergies

    Inform our staff about any food allergies before placing your order.

  • 🧾
    Keep Your Receipt

    Keep your receipt and token until you have collected your complete order.
