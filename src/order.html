Place Your Order in 4 Easy Steps

  1. Choose your items: Check the menu or deals page and decide what you want.
  2. Pay at the counter: Go to the cash counter, tell the staff your order, and pay by cash or card. Keep your receipt safe.
  3. Collect your order token: You will get a token number with your receipt. Wait for your number to be called on the display board.
  4. Collect your food: Show your token at the pickup counter, collect your order, and check it before leaving.

Good to Know

  • Peak hours: 12:30 PM - 2:00 PM. Orders may take 10-15 minutes longer at peak time.
  • Pick-up rules: Collect your order within 15 minutes of your number being called, otherwise it may be kept aside and reheated.
  • Takeaway: Tell the counter staff in advance if you want takeaway packing.
  • Student discount: Student Combo requires a valid student card at payment.
  • Allergies: Please inform staff about any food allergy before ordering.
